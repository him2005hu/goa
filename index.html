About the Resort

About Caravela Beach Resort

Caravela Beach Resort is a luxurious 5-star property nestled along the pristine shores of Varca Beach in South Goa. Spanning 24 acres of lush tropical gardens, the resort offers elegant accommodations, multiple dining options, a spa, and recreational facilities. Known for its Portuguese-inspired architecture and warm hospitality, it provides a perfect blend of comfort and beachfront serenity.

The architecture of the hotel reflects colonial charm, with wooden interiors and vintage decor creating a warm and welcoming ambiance. Every corner of the property is crafted to offer a serene, refined, and memorable experience for couples, families, and travelers seeking luxury in Goa.
